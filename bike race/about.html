OUR STORY

WHERE THE JOURNEY BEGAN

Founded in 2015 by a group of cycling enthusiasts, Bike Race was created with a simple mission: to bring riders together, challenge limits, and create unforgettable experiences on two wheels.

From a small local event to an internationally recognized race, our journey has been nothing short of extraordinary — and we're just getting started.

HOW IT ALL BEGAN

Bike Race was born from a simple idea — to create a world-class cycling event that brings together riders from all walks of life to challenge their limits and share their passion.

What started as a small group of cycling enthusiasts has grown into an international event series known for its breathtaking routes, seamless organization, and unforgettable experiences.
